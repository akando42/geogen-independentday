## The Wallace Line Between Two Different Ecosystem of Animals

Wallace Line is a deep ocean gap with high speed current between Indochina Indonesia and Papa New Guinea. Through out hundred thousands years of evolution, Wallace line has been the natural barrier that is extremely difficult to overcome and stopped most animals from crossing. 

Animals West and East of the Wallace line developed into two very different ecosystems with different set of species. Charles Darwin noticed these huge differences in his writing after collecting samples from these regions. You would find Komodo dragon and Kangaroo in Papa New Guinea Australia while find no traces of these animals just few hundred kilometers away on the West side if the Wallace line. 

Human was the only animal be able to cross the Wallace line while most animal did not. Even when human crossed the Wallace line, they were not able to easily return to the more abundance ecosystem of Indochina resulted in malnutrition adaptations. 

First human groups crossed into Papa New Guinea Australia became much smaller and shorter than other human groups around the globe to survive in the food scarcity desert islandic environment of Australia outback.

The isolation of Native Australian resulted in very little mixing between Native Australian with human groups until much later with the arrival Asiatic Pacifics Islander boat people and the English naval explorers on their large ocean sail ships.

While many ancient human groups did not survive many waves of human evolution cycles and went instinct, Native Australian still survive until modern day and intermixed with modern human in Australia. 

## The British ExConvicts Freezone Beyond The Reach of Japanese Imperial Navy 

Captain James Cook of the British Imperial Navy discovered Australia during his global exploration voyage. After the discovery of Australia, British Empire started to transport ex-convicts and prisoners to Australia colony to work on the development of new settlements for Anglo English here. 

They picked the far side South East region most fertile region of Australia island to build large seaports, railroad and cities. These first settlements later developed into modern day  two famous cities of Melbourne and Sydney. 

Native population in Australia like in the America was quickly reduced in numbers during the British expansion with ex-convicts and criminals from Britain killed off large numbers of Native Australian. 

While British was able to transfer large numbers of aggressive founder population to Australia to establish permanent foothold in Australia, Japanese Imperial navy was not able to do that during World War 2.

During Japanese Imperial expansion with more advanced planes and ocean ships powered by the combustion engine, they quickly captured most of China, Indochina and South East Asian islands. They partially captured the North side of Papa New Guinea but was not able to capture any Australian cities. 

Japanese Imperial Navy aircraft carrier battle groups bombed Darwin, the northern most city of Australia from the air, destroyed most planes and naval ships docking in the port of Darwin but was not able to put boots on the ground. Australian South East far side most populous cities of Melbourne and Sydney was beyond the range of Japanese bombers and left untouched through out the war. 

The large ocean distance from the coast of China and Indochina to Australia was too far for the Japanese Imperial Army and Navy logistics. The Japanese Imperial Navy Aircraft Carrier battle vs the American Navy Aircraft Carriers resulted in a Partial draw with Japanese navy not able to land troops and supplies on Australian shore. 

As the result, there were no intermixing between Japanese and Australian during World War 2 while there were extensive intermixing between Japanese, Chinese and South East Asian population during the War.

Not until the Fall of Saigon and the End of the Vietnam war that we started to see new introduction of East Asian gene groups into the Australian island fortress. 

Australia with very high level of food security, same farmland total area as the Yellow Yangtze delta but much lower population, accepted the Saigon boat people war refugees into Australian cities after the War. 

This small seed of original Vietnamese war refugees developed into a large population of more than 600,000 Vietnamese living in Australia nowadays. They integrated well into the Anglo English Australian society, having access to Australian much higher calories diet became even healthier and better looking than Vietnamese living in Vietnam. 

The success integration between Vietnamese and Australian resulted in more trades and travels along the China, Japan, Korea, Indochina, South East Asian island nations and Australia similar timezone.
